Claims Denial & Revenue Cycle Analysis

Headline Finding

[X%] of denials traced back to just [Y] root causes, all of them fixable upstream of the payer.

The Problem

The organization's claims denial rate had climbed, but the standard denial-reason codes reported by payers were too generic to point to a fixable root cause — most denials were logged simply as 'documentation' or 'coding' issues without further detail, leaving the revenue cycle team unable to target a fix.

The Approach

Denied claims were cross-referenced against the originating encounter data, coding details, and prior authorization records to reconstruct the actual root cause behind each denial, beyond the generic payer-assigned reason code. Denials were then grouped by true root cause — rather than by payer's stated reason — to find which specific, correctable process failures were responsible for the largest share of denied revenue.

The Outcome

Grouping by true root cause turned a vague 'denials are up' problem into a short, prioritized list of specific process fixes the revenue cycle team could own and act on. [Insert your measured reduction in denial rate or recovered revenue once available.]
