Hospital 30-Day Readmission Prediction

Headline Finding

A ranked risk list let a limited-capacity care team focus outreach on the [X%] of discharged patients who accounted for the majority of readmissions.

The Problem

A hospital's post-discharge follow-up team had capacity to call only a fraction of recently discharged patients each day, but no reliable way to decide who to prioritize. Follow-up calls were largely made in discharge order, not risk order — so high-risk patients sometimes waited days for a check-in while lower-risk patients were called first.

The Approach

Historical discharge records were combined with diagnosis codes, prior admission history, length of stay, and discharge disposition to build a 30-day readmission risk model. Feature importance was reviewed with clinical staff to confirm the drivers made clinical sense, and the model's output was translated into a simple daily ranked list rather than a raw probability score, so it could be used without any data science background.

The Outcome

The care team adopted the ranked list as their daily call sheet, prioritizing highest-risk patients first.
