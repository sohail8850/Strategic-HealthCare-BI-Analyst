Provider Network Adequacy Analysis

Headline Finding

Network adequacy looked compliant at the aggregate level, but [X] specific counties fell short of at least one access standard once analyzed individually.

The Problem

A payer needed to confirm their provider network met state and federal adequacy standards — covering distance, appointment wait-time, and provider-to-enrollee ratio requirements. Prior reporting summarized adequacy at the state level, which masked pockets of poor access within specific counties or specialties.

The Approach

Provider location data was geocoded and matched against enrollee zip codes to calculate drive-time and distance to the nearest in-network provider by specialty. This was layered with wait-time survey data and enrollee counts per provider to test all three adequacy dimensions independently, county by county and specialty by specialty, rather than as a single blended score.

The Outcome

The county-level breakdown surfaced specific, addressable gaps that the state-level summary had hidden — giving the network team a prioritized list of where to recruit additional providers or adjust referral patterns.
