Emergency Department Throughput Bottleneck Analysis

Headline Finding

A single stage in the ED patient journey accounted for [X%] of total time-to-discharge — more than any other single step.

The Problem

Average ED length-of-stay had been rising, but leadership didn't have a clear picture of where in the patient journey the time was actually being lost — triage, bed assignment, physician evaluation, lab turnaround, or discharge processing all looked plausible without a stage-by-stage breakdown.

The Approach

Timestamped patient-flow data was reconstructed into a stage-by-stage timeline for each ED visit, measuring time spent at triage, waiting for a bed, waiting for physician evaluation, awaiting lab or imaging results, and final discharge processing. A simple simulation then tested how total length-of-stay would shift under different staffing scenarios, such as adding one additional provider during peak hours.

The Outcome

The stage-by-stage breakdown identified the specific bottleneck driving the overall length-of-stay increase, redirecting the conversation from 'we need more beds' to a more targeted staffing and process fix. [Insert your simulated or measured wait-time reduction once you have final numbers.]
