Care-Gap Prediction for Chronic Disease Management

Headline Finding

Patients flagged as high-risk for a missed follow-up were [X] times more likely to actually miss it than the average patient in the program.

The Problem

A chronic disease management program relied on generic reminder outreach sent to every enrolled patient on the same schedule, regardless of individual risk of disengaging from care. This meant outreach effort was spread evenly across patients who were already reliably attending appointments and patients who were quietly falling out of the care pathway.

The Approach

Appointment history, time since last visit, medication refill patterns, and prior no-show behavior were used to build a model predicting the likelihood a patient would miss their next scheduled screening or follow-up. Patients were segmented into risk tiers so outreach could be matched to risk level — lighter-touch reminders for low-risk patients, and proactive calls for high-risk ones.

The Outcome

Tiered outreach let the care team redirect limited staff time toward the patients most likely to disengage, rather than spreading effort evenly. [Insert your measured change in follow-up completion rate or no-show rate once available.]
