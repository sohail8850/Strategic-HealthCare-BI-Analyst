Electronic Health Records (EHR) / Patient Outcomes Analysis

Headline Finding

Just [X%] of patients accounted for a disproportionate share of total cost and readmission risk in the cleaned dataset.

The Problem

A raw EHR extract contained duplicate patient records, inconsistent coding, missing encounter data, and free-text fields that made any reliable cost or outcomes analysis impossible without significant cleanup first. Leadership wanted to know which patient segments were driving cost and risk, but couldn't trust the data as extracted.

The Approach

A structured data-cleaning pipeline standardized patient identifiers, reconciled duplicate records, normalized diagnosis and procedure coding, and flagged encounters with missing or implausible values for review. Once the dataset was analysis-ready, cost and readmission risk were segmented by diagnosis group, age band, and comorbidity count to identify which patient segments concentrated the most cost and risk.
